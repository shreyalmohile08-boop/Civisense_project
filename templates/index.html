How Civisense Operates

From incident capture to field crew deployment in under 3 minutes.

01

Citizen Capture

Citizens snap a photo, add a one-line description, and let browser GPS pin the exact ward coordinates automatically.

02

AI Vision & Routing

The multi-modal AI categorizes problem type, estimates severity (Low/Med/High), sets priority score, and routes ticket to the right department.

03

Transparent Tracking

Citizens track live status through a 5-stage progress timeline while municipal admins update remediation milestones in real-time.
