AI Verification Protocol

How Our AI Analyzes Your Report

When you press submit, the Civisense inference engine performs 4 instant operations:

  • Vision Classification

    Evaluates visual features (texture, contrast, dark fluid, asphalt edges) to identify category.

  • Severity & Priority Scoring

    Determines urgency rating (Low, Medium, High, Critical) and maps a 1-10 SLA priority score.

  • Department Routing

    Maps issue to Road Maintenance, Solid Waste, Water Board, Electrical Dept, or PWD.

  • Unique CIV-ID Generation

    Generates an instant tracking reference code so you can follow resolution progress 24/7.

Hackathon Tip: Use the Nagpur demo presets or click anywhere on the interactive map to pin coordinates effortlessly!
